  1. Handstand Pose

    2. How to Do It:
    Step 1:From a standing split position, place your palms on the mat directly below your shoulders and gaze at the space between your hands.
    Step 2:Stack the standing foot directly below your hip with a slight bend in the knee.
    Step 3:Engage your core and kick up with control, drawing the thigh up and into the hip socket to lead with the standing leg.
    Step 4:Reach the lifted leg straight up and engage through your toes. Bring your legs together with your big toes touching and heels slightly separated.

  2. Monkey Pose

    3. How to Do It:
    Step 1:From half monkey pose, shift your torso up and place your fingertips directly below your shoulders.
    Step 2:Flex your front foot and engage your quad to protect your knee.
    Step 3:Begin to slide the back knee back and the front heel forward until your front sit bone connects with the ground.
    Step 4:Square your hips as much as possible by rolling the inner thigh of the back leg up.
    Step 5:Inhale to lengthen the spine and exhale to fold over the front leg drawing your ribs and navel in.
    *Note:

    • For extra support, keep your hands down at your sides.
    • For an added challenge, reach your arms up above your head.

  3. One-Legged King Pigeon Pose

    4. How to Do It:
    Step 1:From downward-facing dog, lift one leg high off the ground and bend it at the knee.
    Step 2:Shift forward and bring the lifted leg to the mat behind your hands, laying your shin down perpendicular to your body
    Step 3:Untuck the back toes and lower down to the mat, ensuring that your back leg is extended in a straight line behind you
    Step 4:Square off your hips and bend your straight leg to a 90-degree angle. Knit your shoulder blades together and down to draw the chest forward as you slowly gaze up toward the sky
    Step 5: Extend your arms high and back, reaching for your foot as you lengthen out your neck.
    Step 6: To deepen the hip stretch, release your foot and fold forward, walking your hands out in front of you or coming down onto your forearms.
